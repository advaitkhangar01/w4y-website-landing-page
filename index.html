PRACTICAL SOLUTIONS

Solving Daily Architecture Management Headaches

Traditional software is either too generic or too complex. Here is how W4Y addresses real problems with simple, everyday logic.

THE HEADACHE 01

Staff Clocking In From Home

Employees manual-logging their timestamps or checking in via GPS spoofers while still in bed, leading to inaccurate payroll calculations and zero accountability on construction sites.

THE SIMPLE CURE
Geofenced Site Zones (Clock-in coordinates are locked to office bounds or strict site maps).
THE HEADACHE 02

Unplanned Late Expense Edits

Staff logging, editing, or deleting project expense bills weeks after client accounts have been settled, which results in frustrating bookkeeping corrections and massive tax audit delays.

THE SIMPLE CURE
15-Day Lockout Switch (Operating costs become read-only after 15 days to protect transaction logs).
THE HEADACHE 03

Jumping Ahead on Stage Milestones

Teams rushing to render or build site foundations while initial drawings and planning permits are still pending signature, causing huge planning mistakes and expensive design re-work.

THE SIMPLE CURE
Sequential Milestone Guards (Steps automatically lock if previous checks are incomplete).

COMMON QUESTIONS

Answers in Clear English

Have questions about how W4Y fits into your studio workflow? Here are clear, simple answers.

Still Have Questions? Ask Us
How does location-based attendance work?

When your employees arrive at the office or an active construction site, they click "Check In" inside the app. The system checks their coordinates using their device's built-in GPS. If they are within 100 meters of the designated area, they are logged as present. If they are slightly outside the zone, it logs a warning flag, and if they are off-site, it blocks the check-in to prevent timesheet fraud.

What is the 15-day expense lockout?

To keep your books tidy and ready for tax filings, any expense bill logged by your team is locked permanently 15 days after it is created. Once locked, it cannot be modified or deleted. This ensures your accountant's balance sheets remain accurate and compliant, preventing random historic edits that throw off your monthly taxes.

Can we create invoices for out-of-state clients?

Yes. W4Y automatically calculates and splits your taxes based on where your client is located. For local state clients, it automatically splits the bill into CGST and SGST (9% + 9%). For out-of-state accounts, it combines it into a single IGST (18%) charge. You do not need to do any manual math.

Is my firm's drawing data safe?

Absolutely. All drawing PDFs, receipts, and photos are encrypted and securely stored. With role-based security settings, only staff with "Design Head" or "Admin" clearance can access blueprints, while external subcontractors can only view assigned tasks on their dashboard.
